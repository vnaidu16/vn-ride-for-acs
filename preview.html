The mission

Ironman 70.3: 1.2 mi ocean swim, 56 mi bike, 13.1 mi run. The bike is trending, the run base is real, and the swim distance is done: 2,250 yd continuous on Aug 27 against 2,112 on race day. He is a certified diver, so open water is comfort, not fear. The final block is about holding race pace with the long rides layered on top.
